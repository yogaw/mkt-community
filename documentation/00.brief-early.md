## 1. Product Overview

A web-based investment community platform for a stock market influencer and their existing community.

The initial goal is to provide a dedicated place where members can:

- watch recorded market insight videos
- read news and announcements
- see upcoming live sessions
- access important community updates
- interact with content

> The first version should prioritize **content consumption and community information**, not trading functionality.

---

## 2. Main User Types

### Community Member

The main user of the platform. They should be able to:

- log in
- see the latest content
- watch videos
- read news and announcements
- see upcoming live sessions
- search / filter content
- access their profile

### Admin / Influencer

The content owner. They should be able to:

- publish video content
- publish news
- publish announcements
- manage upcoming live sessions
- manage members
- feature or pin important content

---

## 3. MVP User Navigation

For the initial launch, keep the member navigation simple:

1. **Home**
2. **Videos**
3. **Updates**
4. **Profile**

**Updates** contains both:

- News
- Announcements

> No separate Community page is required yet unless members are allowed to create their own posts.

---

## 4. Home

### Purpose

Give members a quick overview of what is happening in the community.

### Featured Content

Show one important piece of content prominently.

> **Weekly Market Outlook**
> Key market themes to watch this week
>
> **[Watch Now]**

The featured content can be:

- video
- announcement
- important news

### Upcoming Live Session

Show the next scheduled Zoom / Google Meet session.

> **Upcoming Live Session**
>
> **Weekly Market Discussion**
> Wednesday · 19:00 WIB
>
> **[Join Session]**

For MVP, the platform does **not** need to host live streaming. The button can simply redirect to:

- Zoom
- Google Meet
- other external meeting platform

### Latest Videos

Show recent videos. Each card should contain:

- thumbnail
- title
- short description
- duration
- publish date

> **[Thumbnail]**
>
> **Weekly Market Outlook**
> 24 min · Sep 7
>
> What to watch across banking, commodities and IHSG this week.
>
> **[Watch]**

### Latest Updates

Show recent news and announcements.

> 📢 **Announcement** — Live Session Tonight — 2 hours ago
>
> 📰 **Market Update** — Banking Sector Update — Today
>
> 🎥 **Video** — BBCA Deep Dive — Yesterday

---

## 5. Video Library

### Purpose

Allow members to easily browse previously published market videos.

### Features

Members can:

- browse all videos
- search videos
- filter videos by category
- open video detail
- watch video

Example categories:

- All
- Market Outlook
- Stock Analysis
- Education
- Market Recap

---

## 6. Video Detail

Each video page should include:

### Video Player

Main video playback area. The video may come from:

- uploaded recording
- Vimeo
- YouTube unlisted
- another video provider

### Video Information

Show:

- title
- description
- publish date
- duration
- category

> **Weekly Market Outlook**
> Sep 7 · 24 min · Market Outlook
>
> This session covers IHSG, banking stocks and key market themes for the week.

### Optional for MVP — Comments

> **Discussion** — 12 Comments
>
> **John:** Interesting view on BBCA.
>
> **[Add Comment]**

> If timeline is tight, comments can be moved to the next release.

---

## 7. Updates

Updates combines:

- **News**
- **Announcement**

Members can filter between them.

> **Updates**
> `All | News | Announcement`

---

## 8. News

### Purpose

Provide short market information or community-relevant updates.

Each news item should show:

- title
- short summary
- publish date
- optional image
- category

> **MARKET UPDATE**
>
> **Foreign Flow Returns to Banking Stocks**
>
> Foreign investors recorded stronger buying activity across major banking stocks today.
>
> Sep 7

Clicking it opens the full article.

---

## 9. Announcement

### Purpose

Communicate important information directly to members.

Examples:

- live session schedule
- event reminder
- platform maintenance
- new community feature
- special session
- membership information

> 📢 **ANNOUNCEMENT**
>
> **Live Session Tonight**
>
> Join the weekly market discussion at 19:00 WIB.
>
> **[View Details]**

Announcements may contain CTA buttons such as:

- Join Session
- Register
- Read More
- View Schedule

## 15. Main User Journey

The primary experience should be:

```text
Member logs in
      ↓
Sees latest market update
      ↓
Watches latest video
      ↓
Checks upcoming live session
      ↓
Reads community announcement
      ↓
Returns when new content is published
```

That should be the core experience for the first release.

---

## 16. Product Principle

The MVP should feel like:

> **Content Hub + Private Investment Community**

rather than:

> **Social Media + Trading Platform**

For this launch, the core value is:

> One trusted place for members to follow the influencer's market insights, recorded sessions, news, and important community updates.

The stock-market-specific features — such as ticker pages, watchlists, sentiment, community-generated posts, subscriptions, and portfolio tools — can come after this foundation is stable.
